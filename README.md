# OTD-CenterOnKey

Ever sit down at your desk and spend minutes trying to get your tablet and hand into that *perfect* comfortable position again?  
This plugin lets you instantly recenter your tablet area whenever your setup feels off.

# How It Works

When the activation key is pressed, the plugin recenters your tablet area based on your current pen position.

This allows you to:

1. Quickly reposition your active area whenever your setup feels uncomfortable.
2. Fine-tune and discover the perfect tablet coordinates for your personal grip and playstyle.

The calculated coordinates are automatically printed to the console, making it easy to reuse or manually configure your preferred area later.

# How to Use

1. **Download**  
   Grab the latest plugin build from the [Releases](../../releases) section.

2. **Install**  
   Add the plugin to your OpenTabletDriver Plugin Manager.

3. **Change the Output Mode**  
   In OpenTabletDriver, go to **Output** and change the mode from **Absolute** to **CenterOnKey**.

3. **Trigger**  
   Activation hotkey is bound to the **`Y`** key.

4. **Important**  
   Your pen tip **must be touching or hovering near the tablet surface** at the exact moment you press the key for recentering to work.  
   Remember to switch back to **Absolute mode** if you're only using it for finding perfect tablet coordinates.
